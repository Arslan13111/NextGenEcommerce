Delete Your NextGen Store Account

Signed-in users can delete their account in the NextGen Store app from Settings, Privacy & Data, Delete Account.

Request deletion without the app

Send an email from your registered account address to support@nextgenecommerce.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request". Include the email address used for your account.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account is removed.
  • Your saved shipping addresses and device notification token are deleted.
  • Your profile name, phone, email, and profile image link are anonymized.

What may be retained

Order, payment, refund, tax, fraud-prevention, and support records may be retained when required for security, accounting, legal, or dispute-resolution purposes.
