NextGen Store Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 7, 2026

Data We Collect

We collect account details such as name, email, phone, profile photo, saved addresses, order history, payment status, device notification token, and support messages. AR try-on may process photos that you choose to upload.

How We Use Data

We use data to create accounts, process orders, deliver products, send order notifications, provide support, prevent fraud, and improve app reliability.

Payments

Payments are handled through the selected payment provider. We store order payment status and transaction references, but the app should not store full card or wallet credentials.

Sharing

We share only the data needed to operate the service with payment processors, delivery partners, retailers, hosting providers, support tools, and legal or security reviewers when required.

Your Choices

You can edit your profile, delete saved addresses, manage notification preferences, and delete your account from Settings. You can also request deletion at support@nextgenecommerce.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@nextgenecommerce.com.
